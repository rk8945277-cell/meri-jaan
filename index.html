Chapter 01

The beginning

Pata hai baby… 🥹❤️ mujhe aaj bhi woh din yaad hai jab hum dono mile the. Meri jaan, main woh din kabhi nahi bhool sakta, kyunki woh meri life ke sabse khoobsूरत aur special moments mein se ek tha. 🌸💖

Yaad hai, baby, jab tum tilak mein mujhe mala pehnane aayi thi? 🌺🥹 Pehle toh mujhe laga ki shayad woh mala mere liye nahi hai, lekin tum toh mere liye hi laayi thi. ❤️ Tum jab mujhe mala pehnane ki koshish kar rahi thi, toh tumhari height mujh tak pahunch hi nahi rahi thi. Tum panjon par khadi hokar jaise-taise mala pehna rahi thi, aur main tumhara haath pakadkar tumhe dekh raha tha… aur pata nahi main kaise-kaise hans raha tha. 🤭🥰 Us pal se hi, meri jaan, mera dil tumhare liye kuch alag hi mehsoos karne laga tha. 💘🫂

Phir main ghar chala gaya, aur tum apne bhaiya ki baraat lekar mere ghar aayi. 🥹💗 Us din main itna sharma raha tha ki tumse kuch bol hi nahi paaya. Maine bhi socha tha ki main tumhe mala pehnaunga, lekin aisa ho hi nahi paaya. 🤭❤️

Uske 4–5 din baad main tumhare ghar aaya tha. Tum mujhe room mein le gayi thi aur nashta karne ke liye poochh rahi thi, lekin main itna sharmila ho gaya tha ki theek se kuch bol bhi nahi paa raha tha. 🥹😅 Aur baby, tum mere liye Frooti bhi laayi thi, brush bhi… tum kitna care kar rahi thi mera. 🥺🫶 Woh chhoti-chhoti baatein aaj bhi mere dil mein bilkul waise hi sambhal kar rakhi hain. 🧃💗

Meri jaan, woh din mere liye kitne khaas the, jab main tumhe apni aankhon se dekh paata tha. 🥹❤️ Uske baad se lekar aaj tak hum mil bhi nahi paaye, lekin tumhari yaadein mere dil ke bahut kareeb hain. 🌍🫂

Phir main ghar aa gaya aur bahut mehnat-mashakkat karke tumhari ID dhoondhi. 😅💌 Tumhe message karte waqt mujhe bahut darr lag raha tha, phir bhi maine himmat karke message kar hi diya. Uske baad main tumhare reply ka besabri se intezaar kar raha tha. 🥹📱❤️ Jab tumhara message aaya, baby, main bata nahi sakta ki main kitna khush hua tha! Mere haath-pair pata nahi kyun kaanp rahe the. 🤭💓 Woh meri life ke sabse special dinon mein se ek tha, meri jaan… aur tum us yaad ka sabse pyaara hissa ho. 🫂😘🌷

Chapter 02

The Messages That Brought Us Closer 💌

Meri jaan ❤️🥹, jab humne pehli baar messages par baat ki thi na, mujhe bahut darr lag raha tha ki kahin kuch galat na ho jaaye, tum kuch galat na bol do… lekin meri bacchi se toh kuch galat ho hi nahi sakta tha. 🫶🏻💗

Mujhe yaad hai, baby, us waqt mere paas apna phone bhi nahi tha. 📱🥺 Main bas raat hone ka intezaar karta tha ki kab Bade Papa aayenge aur main unse phone lekar tumse baat karunga. Aur kabhi-kabhi toh woh phone hi nahi dete the… us waqt mujhe kitna bura lagta tha, ye main hi jaanta hoon. 😔❤️‍🩹 Bas tumse baat karne ka mann karta tha, meri jaan. ❤️

Hamare rishte ki shuruaat dosti se hui, phir hum best friends bane. 🫂💕 Tumne beech mein mujhe propose bhi kiya tha, lekin maine tumse kaha ki main 12th ke baad reply dunga. 🙈❤️ Lekin jab maine tumhe apne ek male best friend ke saath birthday par itne paas khade hokar photo khinchwate dekha na, toh mujhe bahut bura laga. Gussa bhi bahut aaya aur tumhe khone ka darr bhi laga. 🥺💗 Tab mujhe ehsaas hua ki meri bacchi mere liye kitni special hai aur main tumhe khona nahi chahta.

Phir aakhirkaar, maine bhi apni bacchi ko propose kar hi diya. 🥹💍❤️ Aur bas meri jaan, us din se lekar aaj tak, aur aane wale har kal mein bhi, main chahta hoon ki hamara rishta aise hi pyaar, bharose aur khushiyon se bhara rahe. 🫶🏻💖

Baby, main tumhare baare mein likhne baithun na, toh shayad 4–5 din bhi kam pad jaayenge. 😭❤️ Tum sirf meri girlfriend nahi, meri best friend, meri khushi aur meri zindagi ka sabse pyaara hissa ho. 🥰🧿

I love you so much, meri jaan! ❤️🫂 We are made for each other, and I want to keep choosing you, today, tomorrow, and forever. 💍♾️💗

Chapter 03

Miles Apart, Close at Heart ❤️

Meri jaan, baby, tumhe pata hai maine in 4 saalon mein tumhe kitna yaad kiya hai? 🥺❤️ Main shabdon mein bata hi nahi sakta ki main tumhe kitna miss karta hoon. Jab bhi tumhare baare mein sochta hoon na, meri aankhein bhar aati hain. Kabhi-kabhi toh aisa lagta hai ki pata nahi maine aisa kya kiya hai ki mujhe tumse door rehne ke itne mushkil din dekhne pad rahe hain. 😭💔

Meri bacchi, jab bhi main kisi doosre couple ko saath mein hanste, ghoomte aur ek-doosre ka haath pakadkar chalte dekhta hoon na, toh bas yahi sochta hoon ki meri jaan mere saath kyun nahi hai? 🥹💔 Kaash main bhi tumhara haath pakadkar tumhare saath chal pata, tumhari aankhon mein dekhkar muskura pata aur tumhe apne paas mehsoos kar pata. Pata nahi baby, us waqt kitna rona aata hai. 😭🫂

Meri jaan, main apni feelings tumhe bata bhi nahi sakta. ❤️‍🩹 Kaash tum mere paas hoti na, toh main tumhe bahut zor se apne gale laga leta aur kuch der tak tumhe khud se door hi nahi hone deta. 🫂💗 Main tumhari god mein sir rakhkar sona chahta hoon, tumse dher saari pyaari-pyaari baatein karna chahta hoon aur tumhare saath har woh pal jeena chahta hoon jiska sapna maine in chaar saalon mein dekha hai. 🥹💞

Baby, main tumhare saath kahin door ghoomne jaana chahta hoon, bas tum aur main. 🌍❤️ Main tumhari kamar par haath rakhkar tumhare saath chalna chahta hoon, tumhare saath photos lena chahta hoon, tumhare saath hasna chahta hoon aur har chhoti si khushi ko tumhare saath jeena chahta hoon. 🥰📸💖 Mujhe kisi badi cheez ki zaroorat nahi hai, meri jaan. Bas tum mere paas ho, mera haath tumhare haath mein ho aur tum mere saath muskura rahi ho… mere liye wahi duniya ki sabse badi khushi hogi. 🫶🏻💍

Pata nahi baby, woh din kab aayega jab main tumhe saamne se dekhunga aur apne dil mein chhupakar rakhi saari feelings tumhe ek hug mein bata dunga. 🥹❤️ Kabhi-kabhi Bhagwan se bhi gussa aata hai ki aakhir hamare hisse mein itna intezaar kyun likha hai. Lekin phir khud ko samjhata hoon ki sabr kar, ek din apni jaan se zaroor milega. 🙏🏻

I MISS YOU VERY, VERY, VERY MUCH, MERI JAAN! 😭❤️🫂 Kaash tum abhi mere paas hoti, baby…

I LOVE YOU SO MUCH, MERI JAAN. ❤️🧿 Tumse door zaroor hoon, lekin mera dil hamesha tumhare paas hai. Ek din hum bhi saath honge, baby… aur us din hamari smile sab kuch bayaan kar degi. 🫂💖♾️
